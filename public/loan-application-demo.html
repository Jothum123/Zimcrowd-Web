📚 How It Works

  1. DTNI Calculation: Net Salary × 40% = Maximum Monthly Installment
  2. Reducing Balance: Uses proper compound interest calculations
  3. Tenure Limits: Cold start (90 days), Government (up to 720 days), Others (up to 360 days)
  4. Employment Caps: Government ($300), Others ($100)
  5. Real-time Validation: Instant feedback before submission
