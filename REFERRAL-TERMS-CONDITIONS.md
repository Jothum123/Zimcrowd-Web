You may participate in a Program as an Advocate until the Program is terminated or modified by ZimCrowd. Advocates and Friends will be eligible to receive a specific reward if the qualifying activity is completed before the Program's termination, cancellation, suspension or modification.

To be eligible to receive a reward through a Program, each Advocate and Friend must have (or sign up, in the case of a Friend) a ZimCrowd account in good standing ("Valid Account") at the time the qualifying activity is completed.

An Advocate may refer a Friend by sending the Friend an email, text, WhatsApp message, or social post that includes a unique referral link ("Invitation") to participate in a Program. By participating in a Program, the Advocate agrees to include the Invitation accurately and, in its entirety, when posting/inviting Friends.

Each Friend must complete each of the following steps ("Reward Criteria") by a Program termination or modification date:

1. **Sign up for a Valid Account** for the first time via the link sent by the Advocate. To be eligible, Friend's phone number or email used to sign up cannot have been previously associated with a ZimCrowd account. Signing up for a Valid Account may require certain actions to verify the Valid Account and is subject to the ZimCrowd Terms of Service Agreement; and

2. **Use the ZimCrowd App** to make the qualifying activity as described in the Program (i.e., account opening, loan request, loan approval, loan repayment, loan funding, investment activity).

## Referral Limits

- An Advocate may make an **unlimited number of referrals**; however, the Advocate may only earn a **total maximum rewards credit of $1,000 USD per month**.

- A Friend must not be an existing ZimCrowd customer. A Friend is eligible to receive a Credit by clicking on the referral link provided by the Advocate.

- There is a **limit of one reward credit per Friend per Valid Account**. If two or more Advocates invite the same Friend, ZimCrowd provides a Credit to the Advocate whose Invitation the Friend accepted first, as determined in ZimCrowd's sole discretion.

- If an Advocate has received the monthly limit of $1,000 in Credits, a Friend referred by Advocate prior to Advocate reaching the $1,000 monthly limit will still be eligible to receive a Credit available to Friends.
