# Referral Program Terms (Summary)

## How It Works

**As a Referrer (Advocate):**
- Share your unique referral link with friends
- Earn $5 for each qualifying action your friend completes
- Maximum $1,000 in rewards per month
- Unlimited referrals allowed

**As a Referred Friend:**
- Sign up using a referral link
- Earn $5 for your first loan
- Earn $5 for your first investment
- Must be a new ZimCrowd user

## Earning Rewards

### You Earn When Your Friend:
✅ Receives their first loan - **$5**  
✅ Pays back their first loan - **$5**  
✅ Funds their first loan - **$5**  
✅ Makes their first investment - **$5**

### Your Friend Earns:
✅ Receives their first loan - **$5**  
✅ Funds their first loan - **$5**  
✅ Makes their first investment - **$5**

## Important Rules

- Credits have no cash value
- Credits can only be used for loans and investments
- One reward per friend per account
- Both accounts must be in good standing
- Fraudulent activity results in disqualification
- ZimCrowd may modify or end program anytime

## Using Your Credits

- Applied automatically to your account
- Use for loan payments or funding
- Typically posted same business day
- May take up to 2 business days
