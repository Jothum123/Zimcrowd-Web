## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### 1.1 What is ZimCrowd?
ZimCrowd is a peer-to-peer (P2P) lending platform that connects investors with borrowers in Zimbabwe. Investors provide loans to borrowers and earn interest on their investments.

### 1.2 Key Points
✅ **Potential Returns:** 12% - 36% annual interest  
⚠️ **Risk Level:** Medium to High  
❌ **Not Guaranteed:** Returns are not guaranteed  
❌ **Not Insured:** Investments are not government-insured  
⏰ **Liquidity:** Limited - funds are locked until loan maturity  
📊 **Diversification:** Recommended across multiple loans

## 2. INVESTMENT RISKS

### 2.1 Credit Risk (Default Risk)
**HIGHEST RISK**

**Description:** Borrowers may fail to repay loans.

**Impact:**
- Partial or total loss of principal
- Loss of expected interest
- Collection costs

**Probability:**
- Low Risk Loans: 2-5% default rate
- Medium Risk Loans: 5-15% default rate
- High Risk Loans: 15-30% default rate

**Mitigation:**
- Diversify across multiple loans
- Review borrower credit ratings
- Start with low-risk loans
- Use auto-invest for diversification

### 2.2 Liquidity Risk
**HIGH RISK**

**Description:** You cannot easily withdraw your investment before loan maturity.

**Impact:**
- Funds locked for loan duration (3-36 months)
- Limited secondary market liquidity
- Potential discount if selling early

**Mitigation:**
- Only invest funds you won't need immediately
- Use secondary market (if available)
- Stagger loan maturities

### 2.3 Platform Risk
**MEDIUM RISK**

**Description:** ZimCrowd may cease operations or experience technical issues.

**Impact:**
- Service disruption
- Difficulty accessing funds
- Administrative complications

**Mitigation:**
- Platform is licensed and regulated
- Regular audits and compliance
- Backup servicing arrangements

### 2.4 Economic Risk
**MEDIUM RISK**

**Description:** Economic conditions in Zimbabwe may deteriorate.

**Impact:**
- Increased default rates
- Currency fluctuations
- Reduced borrower capacity

**Mitigation:**
- Monitor economic indicators
- Adjust investment strategy
- Diversify across loan types

### 2.5 Regulatory Risk
**LOW TO MEDIUM RISK**

**Description:** Changes in laws or regulations may affect operations.

**Impact:**
- Platform operational changes
- Tax implications
- Compliance costs

**Mitigation:**
- Platform maintains regulatory compliance
- Regular legal reviews
- Transparent communication

### 2.6 Interest Rate Risk
**LOW RISK**

**Description:** Market interest rates may change during loan term.

**Impact:**
- Opportunity cost if rates rise
- Fixed returns regardless of market

**Mitigation:**
- Stagger loan maturities
- Mix of short and long-term loans
- Regular portfolio review

### 2.7 Fraud Risk
**LOW RISK**

**Description:** Borrowers may provide false information.

**Impact:**
- Higher default probability
- Loss of investment

**Mitigation:**
- Rigorous borrower verification
- Identity checks
- Income verification
- Fraud detection systems
