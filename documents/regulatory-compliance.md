### 3.1 AML Program

**Program Components:**
1. **Customer Due Diligence (CDD)**
   - Identity verification
   - Address verification
   - Source of funds verification
   - Beneficial ownership identification

2. **Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)**
   - High-risk customers
   - Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)
   - High-value transactions
   - Cross-border transactions

3. **Transaction Monitoring**
   - Real-time monitoring systems
   - Suspicious activity detection
   - Pattern analysis
   - Alert generation

4. **Record Keeping**
   - Customer records: 7 years
   - Transaction records: 7 years
   - Compliance records: 7 years
   - Audit trails maintained

### 3.2 Know Your Customer (KYC)

**Individual Customers:**
Required documents:
- National ID or passport
- Proof of address (utility bill, bank statement)
- Proof of income (payslip, bank statement)
- Tax clearance certificate (for high-value)

**Business Customers:**
Required documents:
- Certificate of incorporation
- Business registration
- Tax clearance certificate
- Directors' IDs
- Beneficial ownership declaration
- Financial statements

### 3.3 Suspicious Activity Reporting

**Reporting Obligations:**

- Ongoing monitoring

**Red Flags:**

- High-risk jurisdictions

- Cash-intensive activities
